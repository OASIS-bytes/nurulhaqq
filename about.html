About us

Building a generation rooted in faith, wisdom and service.

Nurulhaqq Quranic and Arabic School exists to guide young learners toward a life of knowledge, integrity and sincere devotion — blending academic excellence with strong Islamic formation.

Our story

Educating hearts and minds in an enriching Islamic environment.

We believe that schools should do more than prepare students for tests — they should prepare them for life. Our curriculum is designed to help each child become a confident believer, a thoughtful learner and a respectful member of society.

From early Quranic learning to advanced Arabic and academic study, every program is built to nurture discipline, compassion, curiosity and purposeful growth. We partner closely with families to support each child's journey.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of balanced Muslim leaders who are spiritually grounded and intellectually equipped.

Our Values

Faith, respect, integrity, discipline and excellence guide everything we do.

Our Community

A caring, motivating environment where parents, students and teachers work together.

Become part of a school community that strengthens both deen and dunya.
