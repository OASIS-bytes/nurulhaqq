About Nurulhaqq

Building a generation rooted in faith, wisdom and service.

Nurulhaqq Quranic and Arabic School, Offa, Kwara State, exists to guide young learners toward a life of knowledge, integrity and sincere devotion — blending academic excellence with strong Islamic formation.

Our school

Educating hearts and minds in a caring Islamic environment.

Nurulhaqq combines authentic Qur'anic and Islamic education with a solid formal academic curriculum. We are dedicated to nurturing every child's faith, character and intellect in one welcoming community.

Our students memorise and understand the Qur'an, develop genuine fluency in Arabic and master core school subjects — supported by caring teachers and a disciplined, values-driven environment.

  • A balanced curriculum: Qur'an, Arabic, academics and character.
  • Experienced, dedicated teachers who know every student.
  • A warm, safe campus designed for focused learning and play.

What we stand for

Our mission, vision and community.

Our Mission

To provide authentic Qur'anic knowledge, Arabic fluency and strong academics in an environment that builds noble character and lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To raise a generation of balanced Muslim leaders who are spiritually grounded, intellectually equipped and morally upright.

Our Approach

Integrated learning where Qur'anic study, Arabic and academics reinforce each other — so knowledge and faith grow together, daily.

Our Community

A caring partnership between teachers, parents and students, supporting every child's growth with patience and encouragement.

Core values

Values that shape everything we do.

These principles guide classroom life, relationships and daily decisions at Nurulhaqq. They are taught, modelled and lived out every day.

Faith (Iman) Trust in Allah and sincerity in action at the heart of learning.
Knowledge (Ilm) Lifelong pursuit of Qur'anic, Arabic and academic knowledge.
Discipline Order, focus and self-control that create space for excellence.
Respect (Adab) Beautiful manners toward Allah, teachers, peers and elders.
Integrity (Amanah) Honesty and responsibility in words, work and relationships.
Excellence (Ihsan) Striving to do one's best in worship, study and service.

Our philosophy

Qur'anic & Arabic education at the centre of learning.

At Nurulhaqq, the Qur'an is not one subject among many — it is the foundation of the school day. Arabic is taught as a living language, opening the way to deeper understanding of the Qur'an and the wider world.

Students memorise with correct tajweed, understand the meaning of what they learn, and practise Arabic in reading, writing and conversation. This strong foundation builds confidence, strengthens faith and enriches every other subject.
