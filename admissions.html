Frequently asked questions

Answers to common admissions questions.

What ages does Nurulhaqq admit?

We admit children from nursery level through to senior secondary level. Placement is based on age and the child's previous learning stage.

Do I need to complete an entrance exam?

For most levels, placement is decided during a consultation and (where appropriate) a simple assessment to place the child at the right stage.

Does Nurulhaqq teach the full academic curriculum?

Yes. Qur'anic and Arabic studies are taught alongside a solid formal curriculum in English, mathematics, science, social studies and ICT.

Can my child transfer from another school mid-year?

Yes, transfers are considered during term time where there is space, with a report card or transfer letter from the previous school.

Is there a tour or visit before enrollment?

Absolutely. Prospective families are welcome to visit the campus during school hours (Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM) to meet the team.

How do I apply?

Simply complete the online application form on the Apply Now page. Our admissions team will then contact you to continue the process.
