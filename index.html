About Nurulhaqq

Building Hearts, Minds and Character Through Knowledge

Nurulhaqq Quranic and Arabic School, Offa, Kwara State, combines authentic Qur'anic and Islamic education with a solid formal academic curriculum. We are dedicated to nurturing every child's faith, character and intellect in one welcoming environment.

Our students memorise and understand the Qur'an, develop fluency in Arabic and master core school subjects — supported by caring teachers and a disciplined, values-driven community.

Our Educational Programs

Structured learning to nurture knowledge, faith and character.

Qur'anic Studies

Qur'an recitation, memorisation, tajweed and foundational Qur'anic knowledge, taught by trained teachers with patience and care.

Arabic Language

Developing students' Arabic reading, writing, vocabulary and communication skills — from foundational sounds to confident fluency.

Islamic Studies

Learning essential Islamic knowledge, manners, values and understanding that guide everyday life with purpose and sincerity.

Western Education

Equipping students with academic knowledge, critical thinking and practical skills to excel in school and beyond.
