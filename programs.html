Academic pathways

Programs designed to grow confident, grounded, and capable learners.

We offer a structured approach to Islamic education and academic development, helping students build excellence in both deen and dunya through every stage of learning.

Foundation

Quranic Foundations

For younger children who are beginning their journey with Salah, Quran reading, memorization, and Islamic manners.

  • Hifz foundations
  • Arabic sound recognition
  • Daily adhkar and manners
Primary

Primary Learning

A balanced curriculum that covers literacy, numeracy, science, and Islamic studies with strong teacher support.

  • Core academics
  • Arabic language exposure
  • Creative confidence-building
Middle

Middle School Track

Builds independence, critical reasoning, leadership, and deeper understanding of faith and knowledge.

  • Project-based learning
  • Debate and presentation
  • Quranic reflection
Advanced

Hifz & Arabic

Focused and rigorous training for students seeking stronger Quran memorization and Arabic fluency.

  • Advanced tajweed
  • Memorization plans
  • Arabic conversation skills
Character

Leadership & Character

Students learn responsibility, empathy, and service through structured mentoring and community engagement.

  • Leadership sessions
  • Community service
  • Behavioral excellence
Support

Student Support

We provide personalized guidance so every learner receives the care and encouragement they need.

  • Learning support
  • Academic mentorship
  • Family partnership
