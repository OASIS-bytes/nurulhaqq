Academic pathways

Programs designed to grow confident, grounded and capable learners.

We offer a structured approach to Islamic education and academic development, helping students build excellence in both deen and dunya through every stage of learning.

Our core programs

Five pillars of a Nurulhaqq education.

Every student benefits from a balanced programme that strengthens faith, language and academics — while building beautiful character along the way.

Qur'an

Qur'anic Studies

Recitation, memorisation (hifdh) and tajweed taught by trained teachers with patience and care — from first letters to confident, beautiful recitation.

  • Correct tajweed & recitation practice
  • Structured memorisation (hifdh) plans
  • Understanding the meaning of what is learned
Language

Arabic Language

Developing Arabic reading, writing, vocabulary and conversation — from foundational sounds to confident fluency used every day in school.

  • Arabic reading & handwriting skills
  • Vocabulary and grammar foundations
  • Conversational practice and immersion
Deen

Islamic Studies

Essential Islamic knowledge, manners and understanding that guide everyday life with purpose, sincerity and beautiful adab.

  • Aqeedah, fiqh & seerah studies
  • Daily adhkar, salah and duas
  • Islamic manners and character lessons
Academics

Academic Education

A strong formal curriculum in literacy, numeracy, science and ICT, equipping students with knowledge, critical thinking and practical skills.

  • English literacy & numeracy
  • Science, social studies & ICT
  • Exam preparation and confidence building
Character

Character Development

Students grow responsibility, empathy and service through mentoring, community engagement and consistent moral guidance.

  • Leadership & responsibility sessions
  • Community service activities
  • Behavioural excellence & mentoring

Learning by stage

A clear journey from foundation to advanced.

Programs are arranged in stages so each child builds steadily on strong foundations at their own pace.

Foundation

Early Qur'anic literacy, basic Arabic sounds, salah readiness, and positive learning habits.

Primary

Core academics join daily Qur'an and Arabic practice, building fluency and confidence.

Middle School

Independent thinking, deeper Qur'anic understanding, leadership and project work.

Advanced Hifdh & Arabic

Rigorous memorisation and Arabic fluency for students aiming for mastery.
